A young woman named Mara Vale leaves a narrowed city life she has learned to make look smaller than it is, from an apartment whose one narrow window faces a brick wall. An invitation from Celia Rowan asks her to come north. Bellwether is a small town of one main street, a bakery, a hardware store, and hills under a very large sky. An imperfect house at the edge of town has a leaking roof and an attic room. The invitation poses the question of whether a horizon can be remembered.
